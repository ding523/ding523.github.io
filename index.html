Hi, I'm Ding Zou. I currently work as a Large Language Model Algorithm Engineer (Team Leader) at ZTE Corporation for the Blue Sword Project, where I lead a research team focusing on Agentic Training research (Algorithm & Infra). I hold a Master's degree in Computer Science and Technology (2021–2024) and a Bachelor's degree in Electronic Engineering (2017–2021), both from Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST).

My research spans multimodal large language models, reinforcement learning post-training, embodied intelligence, and knowledge-aware recommendation.
